# Wrapped NXM

Wrapped NXM (wNXM) is an ERC-20 token that wraps NXM one to one. NXM only transfers between members, so wNXM exists to let the token move freely across wallets and decentralised finance protocols. A member wraps NXM into wNXM, and any holder unwraps wNXM back into NXM once they hold a membership. The [Swap NXM](/using/swap-nxm) guide walks through wrapping in the app.

## Disclaimer

The Wrapped NXM token (wNXM) is not a wrapper created by or supported by Nexus Mutual, and it operates independently. The underlying smart contract governing wNXM is not associated with NXM or Nexus Mutual. A third party developed the smart contract, and it is entirely separate from Nexus Mutual.

The Nexus Mutual user interface offers a wrap and unwrap flow for the benefit of members, but the DAO teams cannot give any assurances or support with regard to the actions or outcomes of the wNXM smart contract. Any engagement with wNXM is done at your own risk. Members are advised to exercise caution and conduct their own due diligence when engaging with wNXM, or to consult the sources listed below for any inquiries related to the token.
