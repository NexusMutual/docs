# Cover wordings and listings

The cover wording is the document a claim is assessed against. It defines what is covered, what is excluded, what evidence is required, and how long after a loss a claim can be filed.

Each product below links the wording the protocol currently records for it. Where a product has public listings, they are listed with their product IDs, and with a link to the listing's annex where it has one. Read the wording for the product you hold before buying and before claiming.
