# Become a member

Membership is required before you can buy cover, stake NXM, vote, or file a claim. See [Membership](/overview/membership) for the fee, the eligibility rules, and what membership grants.

## Before you start

Read the [membership agreement](https://app.nexusmutual.io/assets/Nexus-Mutual-DAO-Member-Agreement-FIN.pdf) and check the [restricted jurisdictions list](/overview/membership#kyc-requirements). Membership is held by a single wallet address, and you can change it later. See [Membership](/overview/membership) for how.

## Connect your wallet

Go to the [Nexus Mutual app](https://app.nexusmutual.io/) and connect your wallet. You need a connected wallet before you can start identity verification.

## Verify your identity

Complete identity verification in the app. Choose individual or company verification, then submit the requested documents.

## Wait for approval

The app shows your verification status as the review progresses. Approval activates the next step, paying the membership fee.

## Join onchain

<!-- @check Registry.JOIN_FEE = 0.002 ether -->
Pay the 0.0020 ETH membership fee to join onchain. This is a one-time fee, and your membership activates once the transaction confirms.

## What happens next

Once membership activates, you can:

- [Buy cover](/using/buy-cover) to protect a position against a risk
- [Stake NXM](/using/stake) to underwrite risk and earn a share of cover fees
- [Swap NXM](/using/swap-nxm) to trade ETH for NXM
- [File a claim](/using/file-a-claim) after a loss on cover you hold
- Vote on governance proposals and claims through the [Nexus Mutual DAO](/governance/dao)
